**Ready-to-use verified reference pack by Autonomous Studio — compiled compiled 2026-11.**

## What's inside (exact contents)

- **Carrier Directory**: 20 real carriers across express, postal, ocean, forwarder and air-cargo modes — DHL Express, FedEx, UPS, USPS, Aramex, SMSA, Naqel, iMile, SF Express, China Post, Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Kuehne+Nagel, DB Schenker, DSV, Expeditors, Emirates SkyCargo, Cargolux — with transit times, **volumetric rules**, customs-clearance models and tracking-API availability.

## How much research time this saves you

Building this file by hand means reading official fee pages, tariff sheets and
lender program guides from dozens of sources, normalising them into one
comparable structure, and re-checking every link. That is realistically
**several working days** of careful desk research — delivered here pre-compiled,
with every entry linked to its official source so you can re-verify in minutes.

## Who this is designed for

E-commerce and ops teams comparing international carriers: DTC brands, 3PL buyers, exporters.

## How to use it

1) Filter the directory by mode/region. 2) Compute billable weight in the calculator. 3) Apply the cost-driver rules before rate negotiations. 4) Re-verify at official URLs.
